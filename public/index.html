How to read these estimates

Grades combine 85% predicted pace and 15% driver preference fit. S+ starts at 98/100. AVG_DRY uses one shared setup and scores it separately on soft, medium and hard tyres.

Uses the parts fitted in the save, including development gains, understanding and chassis limits. Future development is not simulated. Supported saves use Formula entries, a standard car profile and fitted chassis baseline snapshots.

Lap estimates use fresh, fully warmed tyres, 15 kg fuel, push pace, medium engine, balanced spec ERS and neutral tyre-supplier bonuses. Traffic, tyre wear and DRS are excluded.
